Last updated: November 11, 2026

DivvyDup is a product of Startingline HQ, LLC ("we," "us"). This page explains how we use analytics on divvydup.com. For account, payment, and all other information, see the StartingLine HQ Privacy Policy, which also applies to DivvyDup.

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-XSC7C1HZ7Q) to count visits and see how people use divvydup.com, such as which pages are viewed, so we can improve the site. Google Analytics is not set up to receive the expenses or ledgers you enter.

What it collects. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_<ID>, which by default expire after 2 years) to tell visitors apart and keep track of sessions. It also collects information about your device and browser, your IP address, and how you use the site. Google says that when collecting data, Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.

Our settings. Google signals is turned off. Our Google Analytics data retention is set to 14 months. We do not run ads, we do not use Google Analytics for advertising, and we do not sell this data.

How Google uses this data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Changes. If we change how we use analytics, we will update this page and the date above.
